About MoneyMate

MoneyMate is a lightweight, responsive personal finance and savings tracker built to make everyday money management simple, clear, and accessible. Whether you are tracking daily spending or saving up for a specific goal, MoneyMate keeps your total balance, income, expenses, and savings progress organized in one place.

Keeping track of everyday spending and saving up for something important should not require complicated spreadsheets or cluttered apps. MoneyMate was created to give you a clean, distraction-free workspace where your financial numbers are easy to read and update in seconds across desktop and mobile screens.

Key Features

  • Dashboard Overview: View your Total Balance, Income, and Expenses at a glance alongside a visual Goal Progress overview.
  • Transaction Management: Add, edit, and delete income and expense records with descriptions, categories, and dates, using clear green and red styling to scan cash flow quickly.
  • Savings Goals: Create savings goals with target and saved amounts, track completion percentages with progress bars, and update saved amounts quickly with + Add Saved, plus edit or delete goals whenever needed.
  • Account & Data Sync: Sign in or use guest access, customize your profile avatar, cache data locally via localStorage for fast loading, and sync in real time with Firebase Authentication and Realtime Database.
  • Responsive Design: Built with HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript so layouts adapt smoothly from wide desktop screens down to mobile viewports.

How It Works

  1. Sign In or Start Tracking: Open your account or guest session and personalize your profile settings.
  2. Log Income & Expenses: Add transactions on the Transactions page so your balance, totals, and category history stay up to date.
  3. Build Your Savings Goals: Set targets on the Goals page and use + Add Saved whenever you put extra money toward a goal.

Technology Stack

MoneyMate is built using HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript for lightweight client-side performance, paired with Firebase Authentication, Firebase Realtime Database, and browser storage (localStorage and sessionStorage) for reliable data persistence.
